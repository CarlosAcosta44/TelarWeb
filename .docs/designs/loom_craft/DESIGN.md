## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the ethos of the digital atelier—where software architecture is treated not as disposable assembly, but as bespoke structural weaving. Drawing directly from the metaphor of the loom (*telar*), interface elements reflect the interplay of tension, structural warp, dynamic weft, and tactile organic material.

The visual direction rejects both the sterile, cold-spectrum blue-gray templates typical of modern SaaS platforms and the harsh flat black of developer tools. Instead, it pairs a warm obsidian substrate with radiant spun-amber threads, patinated forest accents, and raw linen typographic surfaces.

### Emotional Demeanor & Aesthetic Character
- **Artisanal Rigor:** Measured, deliberate geometry balanced with physical warmth. Micro-details emulate the tactile precision of heritage horology or tailored textiles.
- **Warm Editorial Dark:** Low eye-strain, non-fluorescent warmth constructed on tobacco-tinted charcoal foundations rather than cold synthetic carbon.
- **Tactile Structuralism:** Hairline borders mimic tensioned loom threads; surface elevations feel like layered woven fabric and patinated metals (bronze, raw copper, aged pine).
- **Target Audience:** Visionary engineering leaders, luxury tech patrons, architects, and product founders seeking bespoke web systems with enduring structural and aesthetic value.

## Colors

The palette grounds the interface in deep organic earth and warm mineral tones, illuminated by golden thread work and patinated copper hardware.

### Foundational Canvas & Neutral Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#121110`):** Pure obsidian with an espresso-tobacco undertone. The base frame of the digital loom.
- **Surface Tier 1 (`#1A1816`):** Deep warm charcoal container color for cards, panels, and sidebars.
- **Surface Tier 2 (`#24211E`):** Elevated tactile surfaces, active navigation rows, and floating popovers.
- **Surface Tier 3 (`#2D2925`):** Interactive resting states, code blocks, and inset toolbars.
- **Woven Line / Border (`#3D3730`):** Subtle thread-tension borders, establishing geometric order without stark contrast.

### Brand & Accent Threads
- **Primary Thread — Spun Amber / Raw Ochre (`#D97706`):** Represents the active warp and weft; applied to primary actions, interactive focal points, and brand signifiers.
- **Secondary Thread — Patina Teal / Forest Pine (`#0D9488`):** Evokes aged copper fittings and forest pine; reserved for runtime status, technical architecture nodes, verified metrics, and commit indicators.
- **Tertiary Accent — Terracotta / Loom Copper (`#C25E2E`):** Represents structural stress, alerts, milestones, and high-impact editorial callouts.

### Typography & Ink Levels
- **Parchment Crisp (`#F7F4ED`):** Natural bleached linen. Used for display titles, headlines, and primary figures.
- **Linen Thread (`#E8E3D9`):** Unbleached warm linen for body text, maintaining zero optical vibration against the dark substrate.
- **Spun Dust (`#A8A29E`):** Warm stone gray for secondary metadata, column headers, and structural hints.
- **Muted Thread (`#6E675F`):** De-emphasized indicators, watermarks, and disabled labels.

## Typography

The typographic system fuses the refined geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the structural cadence of **JetBrains Mono**.

### Roles & Execution
- **Headlines & Expressive Display (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Generous apertures and tight tracking evoke the precision of editorial craft publications. Display sizes use negative tracking to form solid textual blocks.
- **Narrative & System Body (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Set in warm linen (`#E8E3D9`) with deliberate line-height multipliers (1.55 to 1.65) to maximize sustained reading comfort across technical essays and specifications.
- **Metadata, Counters, & Code (JetBrains Mono):** Represents the technical loom gears—timestamps, commit shas, architectural dimensions, tags, and status labels are locked into strict monospaced tracking with slight letter-spacing expansions.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an architectural 12-column grid system built around the concept of loom tension: consistent rhythm, deliberate negative space, and aligned warp boundaries.

### Grid & Composition Rules
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column responsive grid with a maximum content container of 1440px, `margin-lg` (4rem) outer margins, and `gutter-lg` (2rem) gutters. Structural sidebars lock to 280px fixed width while primary canvases expand fluidly.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with `margin` (2rem) and `gutter` (1.5rem). Secondary architectural trees collapse into drawer panels.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout utilizing `margin-sm` (1rem) and `gutter-sm` (1rem). Dense data sets wrap into stacked, vertically continuous strands.

### Spacing Architecture
- Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for intimate bindings (tag icons, inline metadata, table row paddings).
- Use `space-md` (16px) for interior card padding, modular field gaps, and compact group divisions.
- Use `space-lg` (24px) for inter-card separation and medium sectional blocks.
- Use `space-xl` (40px) for thematic section breaks and milestone dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system avoids generic floating shadows. Instead, it relies on **Tonal Stratification** paired with **Tension Borders** and **Warm Hearth Glows**.

### Tonal Stratification
1. **Loom Bed (Level 0):** Canvas `#121110`. Deepest recess.
2. **Woven Structural Plane (Level 1):** `#1A1816` with a crisp `1px` border in `#3D3730`. Standard container elevation.
3. **Mounted Tension Shuttle (Level 2):** `#24211E` with subtle top hairline highlight (`rgba(247, 244, 237, 0.08)`).
4. **Suspended Overlay / Popover (Level 3):** `#2D2925` enclosed in an amber-tinted hair-border (`rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.3)`).

### Ambient Hearth Glows
When floating modals or active drawers require absolute spatial detachment, use diffused amber-tinted ambient occlusion rather than pitch-black dropshadows:
`box-shadow: 0 12px 36px -4px rgba(18, 17, 16, 0.8), 0 0 24px 0 rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.06);`

### Hairline Warp Dividers
Sections are separated by ultra-fine horizontal lines featuring gradient fadeouts:
`linear-gradient(90deg, rgba(61, 55, 48, 0) 0%, rgba(61, 55, 48, 1) 50%, rgba(61, 55, 48, 0) 100%)`.

## Shapes

The geometric form language is **Soft (Level 1)**, reflecting precisely planed timber and tensioned weaving frames. Soft radii (`0.25rem` / 4px default, `0.5rem` / 8px for cards, `0.75rem` / 12px for modal envelopes) provide micro-softness while preserving clean architectural silhouettes.

Pill shapes are strictly restricted to contextual status beads and monospaced telemetry chips. Structural interaction zones retain disciplined rectangular silhouettes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Spun Ochre Shuttle):** Background `#D97706`, text `#121110` (weight 600). Hover shifts to `#F59E0B` with a subtle amber corona (`0 0 16px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.35)`). Active state applies a 0.98 scale transform.
- **Secondary (Tension Frame):** Background `#1A1816`, border `1px solid #3D3730`, text `#E8E3D9`. Hover state shifts border to `#D97706` and text to `#F7F4ED`.
- **Ghost (Thread Action):** Background transparent, text `#A8A29E`. Hover reveals `#24211E` background with `#F7F4ED` text.
- **Technical Action:** Background `rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.4)`, text `#2DD4BF`. Used for terminal triggers, deployment actions, and commit operations.

### Cards & Craft Surfaces
- Constructed with `#1A1816` backgrounds and a perimeter thread border of `1px solid #3D3730`.
- Cards feature an optional subtle top-edge warm bronze thread highlight: `border-top: 1px solid rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.3)`.
- Internal card padding strictly observes `space-lg` (24px) for desktop and `space-md` (16px) for mobile.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Beads:** Rendered in `label-sm` (JetBrains Mono).
- **Active Thread / Building:** Background `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.15)`, text `#F59E0B`, border `1px solid rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.3)`. Contains a pulsing 6px amber orb.
- **Integrity Verified / Patina Pine:** Background `rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.15)`, text `#2DD4BF`, border `1px solid rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.3)`.
- **Architectural Tag:** Background `#24211E`, text `#A8A29E`, border `1px solid #3D3730`, roundedness `0.25rem`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Field Canvas:** Background `#121110`, border `1px solid #3D3730`, text `#F7F4ED`, placeholder `#6E675F`.
- **Focus Mechanism:** Border shifts to `#D97706` accompanied by an inner ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #D97706, 0 0 12px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp-cornered square/circle frames in `#121110` with `1px solid #3D3730`. Checked state fills with `#D97706` bearing a dark parchment `#121110` checkmark glyph.

### Lists & Data Strands
- Alternating rows avoid striped zebra fills; they rely on single-pixel hairline dividers (`#24211E`).
- Row hover triggers an amber thread highlight along the left perimeter: a `2px` vertical bar of `#D97706` sliding in alongside a background transition to `#1A1816`.

### Specialized Studio Components
- **Loom Warp Progress (Deployment Rail):** A stepped multi-thread stepper component where unfinished stages appear as faint vertical lines (`#3D3730`), active work appears as a radiant pulsing amber thread (`#D97706`), and completed milestones resolve into solid patina teal (`#0D9488`).
- **Code & Syntax Weaver:** Embedded terminal windows bound by `#1A1816`, with line numbers rendered in muted thread (`#6E675F`) and active execution lines backlit with `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.08)`.
